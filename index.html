How Scene3D works

This page is the master. It holds the 3D scene. Every camera you add here is a screen: open its link on any browser and it shows exactly what that camera sees, in 16:9.

  • Same computer (other tabs or windows of this browser): connects instantly, no internet needed.
  • Other computers: connect over the internet through the room name shown in the panel.
  • Screens draw the scene themselves; the master only sends positions, so they stay sharp and smooth.

On a screen page

Double-click or press F for fullscreen. Add ?fit=window to fill a non-16:9 display.

CAVE mode

Turn on CAVE · 3 monitors: screens 1, 2, 3 become the left, centre and right monitor of a stereo window with head-tracked parallax. On a screen page, T starts webcam head tracking from that window and S swaps the eyes for that screen.
